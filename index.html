01 / A little introduction

A mind for how things work.

I'm currently studying Software Engineering at Usmanu Danfodiyo University, Sokoto. My curiosity about technology keeps growing with every new thing I learn.

I enjoy exploring how software is put together: the decisions behind an interface, the logic behind a feature, and the small details that make something easier to use.

I'm still at the beginning of my journey, and I'm happy to say so. I want to keep asking questions, practising the fundamentals, and improving through the things I build along the way.

My goal is to grow into a skilled software engineer who approaches problems thoughtfully and builds with purpose.
