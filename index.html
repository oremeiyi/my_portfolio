01 / A little introduction

A mind for how things work.

I'm a Software Engineering student at Usmanu Danfodiyo University, Sokoto. I'm especially interested in how a good interface, thoughtful engineering, and online safety come together.

I like seeing an idea move from a rough sketch to a working page. Each project gives me a chance to learn something new and notice what makes an experience clearer for the person using it.

I'm early in the journey, so I'm focused on the fundamentals: practising, asking questions, and getting comfortable with the tools behind modern web projects.

I'm sharing the process as I go—what I'm learning, what I'm making, and what I'd do differently next time.
